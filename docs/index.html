Scoring formula

opportunity = (G_d / G_s) × √P_d × M G_d demand growth = geomean(wiki_yoy, trends_yoy) wiki_yoy = Wikipedia views, last 12 complete months ÷ previous 12 trends_yoy = geomean over keywords of (mean last 52 wks ÷ mean previous 52), each keyword queried alone so it keeps full 0–100 resolution G_s supply growth = geomean over keywords of (videos_recent + 1) ÷ (videos_prior_year + 1) P_d demand size = percentile rank of mean z( log wiki views, log Trends level, log Reddit subs ) M money weight = niche mid RPM ÷ median mid RPM (1.0 while pending)

Why this shape

Demand divided by competition is the core of keyword opportunity scores such as the Keyword Effectiveness Index. The target is the high-demand, low-supply quadrant from Parrot Analytics' demand vs supply matrix. The √P_d term damps tiny niches: with no demand there is no opportunity, however low the competition.

Known limits

YouTube totalResults is an estimate, and YouTube stands in for still-image platforms that have no free count API. Wikipedia pageviews track search interest but weight broad topics heavily. Google Trends is read through pytrends, which is archived and often rate-limited. Reddit gives a single snapshot, so posting rate but not its growth.
